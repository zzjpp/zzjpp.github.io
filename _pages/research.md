### Online Kernel Methods for Large-Scale Nested Simulation

**School of Management, Fudan University**  
March 2026 – Present

**Advisor:** Prof. Jianqiang Hu

- Developed a one-pass kernel method using growing orthonormal dictionaries, recursive ridge regression, and first-order bias correction, without revisiting past observations.
- Established asymptotic normality and mean-squared-error guarantees for smooth nonlinear functionals, with sublinear storage and subquadratic computational complexity.

Selected Project
======

### Graph-Grounded Bayesian Inference for Uncertainty-Aware LLM Evaluation

- Developed a Bayesian framework combining six LLM judges with semantic argument graphs to quantify evaluation uncertainty.
- Established approximate posterior error bounds and designed adaptive stopping rules, reducing retained judge reports by **31.78%** in retrospective experiments.
- Evaluated **1,800 responses** across mathematics, coding, and planning. On an AlpacaFarm subset, the method achieved **86.36% pairwise accuracy**, compared with **77.27%** for direct scoring.
