Beyond Curvature Bias: Higher-Order Manifold Fitting via Multiscale Cancellation

Zhijun Zhu, Haoyu Yang, Ruoyu Xiong, Bingjie Li, and Zhigang Yao

arXiv preprint · November 12, 2026

arXiv:2611.16235 · PDF

Overview

Local smoothing reduces noise but also displaces a fitted manifold because of curvature. We develop geometric bias cancellation (GBC), a framework that combines fits at multiple scales to remove this systematic error. A key step is matching points across fitted manifolds using estimated normal directions, so that bias can be canceled without knowing the true geometry.

Main results

  • Under the stated regularity and Gaussian-noise assumptions, GBC achieves fourth-order population Hausdorff accuracy while preserving a smooth embedded manifold diffeomorphic to the underlying manifold.
  • We establish noise-dependent bias expansions and finite-sample recovery guarantees for successive tangent-space projection and Gaussian density ridges.
  • For a local contraction estimator, we identify why scalar cancellation can fail and develop a directional correction with fourth-order population accuracy and a uniform positive-reach guarantee.
